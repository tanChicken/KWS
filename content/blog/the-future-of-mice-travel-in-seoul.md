Seoul has quietly become one of Asia's most compelling MICE destinations. Beyond the flagship convention centres of COEX and DDP, a new generation of venues — rooftop hanok pavilions, riverside cultural complexes, converted industrial spaces — is redefining what a corporate program can feel like.

## Hybrid by Default

Post-pandemic, every serious event brief includes a hybrid component. Seoul's fibre infrastructure and venue-side production teams make simultaneous in-person and remote programming genuinely seamless, not an afterthought.

## Sustainability as a Selling Point

Corporate buyers increasingly ask for measurable sustainability commitments. From zero-waste catering in Seongsu to carbon-offset transfer fleets, we help planners build programs that read well in a board report — and feel even better on the ground.
