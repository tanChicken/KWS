# Korea Tours by KOREA WITH SUE (KWS)

Curated destination programs designed and operated by our team in Korea. Every itinerary below is a starting framework — durations, hotel grades, inclusions, and routing are fully tailored to each client brief.

---

## 1. Seoul Heritage & Culture Discovery (5D4N)

*Cultural Experiences · Group & FIT*

A foundational journey through Korea's capital, blending royal heritage with the rhythms of modern Seoul.

**Day 1 — Arrival**
Incheon Airport meet-and-greet, private transfer to hotel in Myeongdong. Welcome dinner: Korean BBQ with a hanbok-clad host introducing dining etiquette.

**Day 2 — Royal Seoul**
Gyeongbokgung Palace with the Royal Guard Changing Ceremony, National Folk Museum, Bukchon Hanok Village walking tour with a licensed cultural guide, Insadong for traditional tea and craft shopping. Evening: Nanta non-verbal performance.

**Day 3 — Spiritual & Scenic Seoul**
Jogyesa Temple, Jogyesan templestay introduction, N Seoul Tower and Namsan cable car, Ihwa Mural Village. Dinner cruise on the Han River.

**Day 4 — Living Seoul**
DMZ half-day tour (Imjingak, Third Tunnel, Dora Observatory), afternoon at Dongdaemun Design Plaza and Gwangjang Market for street food. Farewell dinner: traditional Korean royal court cuisine (hanjeongsik).

**Day 5 — Departure**
Hotel check-out and transfer to Incheon Airport.

## 2. Korea Grand Classic — Seoul, Jeonju & Busan (8D7N)

*Group & FIT · Cultural Experiences*

The signature cross-country circuit linking the capital, the spiritual south, and the coastal city.

**Days 1–3 — Seoul:** Gyeongbokgung, Bukchon, Myeongdong, Hongdae, DMZ, Han River cruise.
**Day 4 — Seoul → Jeonju (KTX):** Jeonju Hanok Village, bibimbap cooking class, hanji paper workshop, overnight in a traditional hanok guesthouse.
**Day 5 — Jeonju → Gyeongju:** Bulguksa Temple (UNESCO), Seokguram Grotto, Tumuli Park royal tombs, Cheomseongdae Observatory.
**Day 6 — Gyeongju → Busan:** Haedong Yonggungsa Temple, Gamcheon Culture Village, Jagalchi Fish Market.
**Day 7 — Busan:** Haeundae Beach, BIFF Square, Beomeosa Temple, farewell dinner at a hanwoo grill house.
**Day 8 — Departure** from Gimhae International Airport.

*Cultural Experiences · FIT*

**Day 3 — K-Drama Locations:** Petite France and Nami Island (filming sites for *Winter Sonata* and many recent dramas), Garden of Morning Calm.
**Day 4 — Style & Beauty:** Olive Young flagship, dermatology clinic consultation (optional), personal color analysis, Apgujeong-Rodeo styling tour, Gangnam dinner.
**Day 5 — Studio & Show:** SBS or KBS studio visit (subject to schedule), optional live music show recording attendance, farewell dinner in Itaewon.
**Day 6 — Departure.**

## 7. Winter Korea — Ski & Snow Festival (6D5N)

*Group & FIT · Cultural Experiences*

Seasonal program (December–February) combining alpine resort time with capital sightseeing.

**Day 1 — Arrival in Seoul.**
**Day 2 — Seoul → Pyeongchang/Yongpyong Resort:** Transfer to slope-side accommodation, ski/snowboard rental and lesson with English-speaking instructor.
**Day 3 — Full Ski Day:** Lift pass, lunch on the mountain, optional snow tubing for non-skiers, evening Korean BBQ.
**Day 4 — Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival** (January) or Nami Island winter walk and Garden of Morning Calm Lighting Festival, return to Seoul.
**Day 5 — Seoul:** Gyeongbokgung in snow, hanbok rental for photos, Myeongdong shopping, jjimjilbang (Korean spa) experience.
**Day 6 — Departure.**

## 8. Korea Culinary Journey (6D5N)

*Cultural Experiences · FIT · Luxury Travel*

A food-led itinerary across the peninsula's most distinctive regional cuisines.

**Day 1 — Seoul Arrival:** Welcome dinner at Gwangjang Market night food tour with a local food writer.
**Day 2 — Seoul Foundations:** Kimchi-making class at the Museum of Kimchi, lunch at a hanjeongsik royal court restaurant, afternoon café tour through Seongsu, dinner at a Michelin-recommended modern Korean restaurant.
**Day 3 — Jeonju (Day Trip via KTX):** Bibimbap origin tasting, makgeolli rice wine brewery tour, hanok village street food crawl.
**Day 4 — Andong & Hahoe Village:** Andong jjimdak cooking, Hahoe Folk Village (UNESCO), soju distillery tour at the Andong Soju Museum.
**Day 5 — Busan by KTX:** Jagalchi Fish Market early-morning tour, hoe (raw fish) lunch, dwaeji gukbap (pork soup) tasting, farewell dinner at a hanwoo specialist.
**Day 6 — Departure from Gimhae International Airport.**
